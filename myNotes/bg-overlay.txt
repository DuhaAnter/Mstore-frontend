-- inset-0 is a Tailwind shortcut that sets all four positioning properties to 0.

This:
"absolute inset-0"
is equivalent to:
position: absolute;
top: 0;
right: 0;
bottom: 0;
left: 0;
- Because of inset-0, the child stretches to fill the entire parent.
